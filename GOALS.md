# Goals

Engine: **Unreal Engine** (decided). UEFN-only prizes (Walkie-Talkie, UEFN Best Overall) are out.

A submission can win **one main prize + one modifier** (Rule 13). We aim to be eligible
for every modifier listed here and let the judges pick which one.

## Main prize: Best Overall, Unreal Engine ($7.5k / $5k / $2.5k)

Each criterion is scored 1–5. Ties are re-scored with Fun Factor counted double.

- [ ] Ambition and innovation: something you couldn't usually build in four days
- [ ] Theme: a fresh take that's obvious from the first minute
- [ ] Fun factor (the tiebreaker)
- [ ] Gameplay: original mechanics that feel good
- [ ] Visuals: a strong, consistent look
- [ ] Level design and progression: spaces that guide the player
- [ ] Audio: SFX and music, not placeholder silence

## Main prize alt: Best Student Project ($7.5k / $5k / $2.5k)

- [ ] Only if **every** member is currently enrolled in higher education. TBD per member.

## Modifiers (we can win at most one)

- [ ] **Best Team ($5k)**: we qualify as a team of 2+. Nothing extra needed.
- [ ] **Glass Box ($2.5k)**: dev log link in the entry form, submitted before the deadline
  - [ ] Private prep log running before Nov 12 (also serves as our pre-jam declaration)
  - [ ] Public dev log posts during Nov 12–15, with process, agents, prompts, what broke
- [ ] **Render Royalty ($2.5k)**: best visuals, photoreal or stylized
- [ ] **Pocket Powerhouse ($2.5k)**: best mobile game, needs an Android or iOS packaged build
  - [ ] UNVERIFIED: can one entry ship desktop and mobile builds? Ask in the UEFN Discord before Nov 12.

## After the jam

- [ ] Development Continuation Grant (up to $50k): by invitation, based on how the entry places

## Ship gates (missing one = disqualified)

See the checklist in [README.md](README.md). The ones most likely to catch us:
- [ ] Test-package a build on day 2, not day 4
- [ ] Packaged zip `TeamName_GameName.zip` under 1 GB, uploaded to itch
- [ ] 1920x1080+ gameplay video, 30–60 s
- [ ] Submitted by a human before **Nov 15, 8:59 PM EST**
